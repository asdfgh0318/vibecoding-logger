## Findings worth remembering

- **Vendor test data is not permission.** Cobra publishes an APC 6x5.5-E at 28,781 rpm
  (15% over APC's 25,000) and 7x4-SF rows to 12,825 rpm (38% over the 9,286 SF limit).
- **APC 7x5EP + Cobra CM-2208/34 on 4S** is the only combination found that is safe at
  full charge with no throttle limit (2,671-12,006 rpm measured).
- **AXI 2217/12 is 2-3S only** (not 4S), 32 A / 330 W, 3.17 mm shaft -> 5 mm via PH317.
  It cannot drive the three slow-flyers at any rated cell count, and will not mount the
  NEO 8in (6 mm bore).
- **Drive Calculator stores the WRONG constant for APC E props** - 190,000/D (the glow /
  Speed 400 rule) instead of Thin Electric's 150,000/D. Uniformly 27% over-permissive on
  six props here. Its APC MR props store MaxRPM = 0, so no warning ever fires. Its SF
  data IS correct (9x3.8SF and 9x6SF = 7,222). Found by querying its SQLite DB directly.
- **eCalc rejected**: "The max. physical RPM are not monitored." Its setupFinder is
  fixed-wing only, paid, and has no RPM column. **Drive Calculator** is the only free
  tool doing true prop->motor search ("Match prop"); **Tyto Robotics** is the only one
  that filters by resulting RPM.
- Naming corrections: APC has no 7x5P or 6x4P (they are 7x5EP / 6x4EP); 5x4.5EP exists
  only inside the B5x4.5E-B4 bundle; NEO props are by **Encore RC**, not Invertix or
  Curtis Youngblood.
